# Content Production

Professional content execution pipeline designed for high-quality, SEO-optimized, and brand-aligned writing.

## How This Skill Works

### Mode 1: Research & Brief
1.  **Competitive Content Analysis:** Analyze top-ranking content for the target keyword.
2.  **Source Gathering:** Collect statistics, quotes, and primary sources.
3.  **Produce the Content Brief:** Detailed outline with word count, target audience, and primary/secondary keywords.

### Mode 2: Draft
1.  **Outline First:** Build the structure before the sentences.
2.  **Intro Principles:** Hook the reader and state the "what's in it for me".
3.  **Section-by-Section Approach:** Draft the body content following the brief.

### Mode 3: Optimize & Polish
1.  **SEO Pass:** Natural keyword integration and semantic coverage.
2.  **Readability Pass:** Simplify complex sentences and improve flow.
3.  **Meta Tags:** Generate title tags and meta descriptions.

## Quality Gates — Don't Publish Until These Pass
- [ ] Primary keyword in H1 and first 100 words?
- [ ] At least 3 primary sources/stats cited?
- [ ] No "fluff" sections (every paragraph adds value)?
- [ ] Clear call to action (CTA) at the end?
