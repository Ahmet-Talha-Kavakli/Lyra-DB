# Content Strategy

Strategic planning layer for content that drives business results through pillars and clusters.

## Before Planning
Read the project context to understand:
1.  **Business Context:** What are the conversion goals?
2.  **Customer Research:** Who are we writing for?
3.  **Competitive Landscape:** What are competitors ranking for?

## Output Format

### 1. Content Pillars
Identify 3-5 broad themes the brand should own (e.g., "AI Ethics", "Mental Health Tech").

### 2. Priority Topics
List specific keywords and topics with high business value and achievable difficulty.

### 3. Topic Cluster Map
Map "Pillar" pages and "Cluster" content to build semantic authority.

## Proactive Triggers
- **No topic clusters documented:** Fragile SEO strategy.
- **Content calendar < 4 weeks out:** Execution is reactive.
- **High-volume keywords only:** Missing low-funnel intent.
